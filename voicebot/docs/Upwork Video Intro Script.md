# Upwork Video Introduction Script

Dr. Aqib Mumtaz, Head of AI Research, BitLogix. About 270 words, roughly 1 minute 50 seconds.

## 1. Hook (~10 sec)

Hi, I'm Dr. Aqib Mumtaz. I help companies turn AI ideas into working products: systems that run in production, not just demos.

## 2. Credibility (~25 sec)

I'm Head of AI Research at BitLogix. I have a PhD in AI, peer-reviewed publications in Springer and Oxford journals, and more than ten years of hands-on engineering, from mobile apps and full-stack systems to leading AI teams. I understand both the research and the product side, so I can take a project from first idea to deployment.

## 3. What I Build (~45 sec)

Here's what I can build for you:

- **Speech and voice AI:** speech-to-text, voice bots and call analysis, including Urdu, Roman Urdu and other low-resource languages.
- **LLM applications:** chatbots, AI assistants, RAG systems and document automation, on the cloud or fully on your own hardware for privacy.
- **Healthcare AI:** I've built an AI radiologist for X-ray analysis and a clinical platform that turns doctor–patient conversations into medical notes.
- **Computer vision:** real-time CCTV anomaly detection, object detection and video analytics.

## 4. How I Work (~25 sec)

I start by understanding your business problem, not just the tech. Then I give you a clear plan, regular progress updates and measurable results. I benchmark everything on your real data, so you know exactly how well the system performs before it goes live.

## 5. Call to Action (~15 sec)

If you have an AI project in mind, or just a problem you think AI could solve, send me a message. I'm happy to discuss it and suggest the best approach, even before we start.

Looking forward to working with you!

## Recording Tips

- **Length:** Upwork recommends 1–2 minutes. The first 10 seconds matter most, so keep the hook short.
- **B-roll:** during section 3, show quick clips of the voice bot, CCTV detection and X-ray tool.
- **Pick a niche:** to target one area such as voice AI, spend more of section 3 on it and cut the others.
- **PhD wording:** if your field is Computer Science, say "PhD in Computer Science, specializing in AI."
- **Client names:** say "a healthcare lab" or "an enterprise client" unless you have permission to name them.
